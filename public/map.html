Tips

APP SUPPORT

Below are a list of quick tips to help you get the most out of our Visit First Monday Finder App.

SHOW WHERE YOU ARE

Click the black navigation icon in the upper right of the map to activate and share your GPS location with the app. Be sure to tap "Allow" when your phone prompts you to share your location.

SAVE TO FAVORITES

When you find and tap a location dot or vendor space that you want to find again, save it to your favorites by tapping the star icon in the header. It will turn yellow and be saved to your favorites list in the shop categories drawer.
