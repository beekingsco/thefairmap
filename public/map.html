APP SUPPORT

Below are a list of quick tips to help you get the most out of our Visit First Monday Finder App.

SHOW WHERE YOU ARE

Click the Black Nav icon in the upper right of the map to activate and share your GPS location with the app. Be sure click "Allow" when your phone prompts you to share your location.

SAVE TO FAVORITES

When you find and click on a location dot or vendor space that you want to be sure to find again, you can save it to your favorites by clicking the star icon in the header. It should turn yellow and will be saved to a small list of favorite locations in the "shop categories" list.
