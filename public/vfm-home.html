What Makes First Monday So Special?

Canton First Monday Trade Days is more than a market — it is an experience. Treasure hunters, makers, and deal-seekers explore thousands of vendors offering antiques, handmade goods, boutique fashion, collectibles, and food. Every visit is a chance to uncover hidden gems and be part of a tradition that has drawn guests for more than 150 years.
