What Makes First Monday So Special?

With endless shopping options available today, Canton First Monday Trade Days stands apart as more than just a market, it's an experience. As the World's Largest Flea Market, it's where treasure hunters, trendsetters, and deal-seekers come together to explore thousands of vendors offering unique antiques, handcrafted goods, boutique fashion, rare collectibles, and mouthwatering food.

Here, every time you Visit First Monday is an adventure, a chance to uncover hidden gems, meet passionate makers, and be part of a rich tradition that has been drawing visitors for over 150 years. Whether you're searching for one-of-a-kind finds or just soaking in the energy of this legendary marketplace, you'll never regret it when you Visit First Monday. It truly is a place you won't find anywhere else.

VisitFirstMonday.com is your official guide to First Monday Trade Days in Canton, Texas, the largest and longest-running flea market in the USA. The market happens only on the Thursday, Friday, Saturday, and Sunday before the first Monday of each month.

Lewis side parking

Start on the Lewis side: $5 parking and closer access.

Visit First Monday is highlighting the Lewis side of Canton because it is the side we are mostly marketing. Lewis First Monday lists $5 parking at North Gate, East Gate, Dealers Row, and Arbors Entrance, with short walks to Lewis pavilions and Arbors.

Lewis First Monday: 801 N. Trades Day Blvd, Canton, TX 75103 · 903-567-1234
