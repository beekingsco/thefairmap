Where to park

This flyover shows the First Monday grounds, the highway, and the lots beside the buildings. Use Exit 527 for the closest $5 parking off Highway 19.

Exit 527, then the Lewis side

From I-20, take Exit 527 onto Highway 19 (Trade Days Boulevard). The closest posted $5 lots on the Lewis side are a short walk into the market.

  • North Gate — $5. 109 County Road 2106, Canton, TX 75103.
  • East Gate — $5. 252–282 N Trade Days Blvd, Canton, TX 75103.
  • Dealers Row — $5. 700–710 N Trade Days Blvd, Canton, TX 75103.
  • Arbors Entrance — $5. 336–358 N Trade Days Blvd, Canton, TX 75103.

Prices and access can change. Follow the lot signs and attendants when you arrive. Lewis First Monday: 801 N. Trades Day Blvd, Canton, TX 75103 · 903-567-1234.
