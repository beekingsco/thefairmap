Stay planning answers

Frequently asked questions

When should I book for First Monday?

First Monday weekends can fill quickly, so book early once you know which monthly market you want to attend. Spring and fall weekends can be especially popular.

Can I stay in an RV near the market?

Yes. First Monday RV Park offers full-hookup sites. Reserve through the official online booking link above or call 903-567-6556 for booking help.

Where can I compare hotels and other lodging?

Use Visit Canton's current lodging directory to explore hotels and other stays in and around Canton, then confirm availability and policies directly with your chosen provider.

What days is the market open?

The market takes place Thursday through Sunday before the first Monday of each month. Monday is in the name, but it is not a market day. Always check the current dates before traveling.
