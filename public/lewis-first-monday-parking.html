Lewis-side $5 parking lots

North Gate $5

Lewis lists North Gate at 109 County Road 2106, Canton, TX 75103. Posted walk times include about 2 minutes to Pavilion 4500 / 4000, 3 minutes to Pavilion 2 / 1, and 6 minutes to Arbors.

East Gate $5

Lewis lists East Gate at 252–282 N Trade Days Blvd, Canton, TX 75103. Posted walk times include about 1 minute to Arbors, 3 minutes to Pavilion 1, and 4–6 minutes to other pavilions.

Dealers Row $5

Lewis lists Dealers Row at 700–710 N Trade Days Blvd, Canton, TX 75103. Posted walk times include about 2 minutes to Pavilion 1 / 2 and 3 minutes to Pavilion 4000 / 4500.

Arbors Entrance $5

Lewis lists Arbors Entrance at 336–358 N Trade Days Blvd, Canton, TX 75103. Posted walk times include about 1 minute to Arbors and 2–5 minutes to the pavilions.

Always follow current signs, attendants, and official lot instructions when you arrive. Prices and access can change, so this page uses LewisFirstMonday.com as the source for the Lewis-side parking facts.
